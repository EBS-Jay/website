Frequently Asked Questions

Who is a good fit for The Owner’s Boardroom?

You own and actively run a business that’s been operating for at least two years. You make decisions for the business, can commit to attending monthly, and are willing to share what you’ve learned with other owners.

How do you decide who gets a seat?

Start by applying online. The Elevating Business Solutions team reviews your application and decides whether to invite you for an interview. We’ll talk about what you’re working through, what you want from the group, and what experience you’d bring. Our team makes the membership decision after the interview.

Could a competitor join my chapter?

Each chapter accepts only one business per industry. That gives you room to talk openly about decisions you’d hesitate to discuss in front of a competitor.

How can owners from other industries help me?

The details of your business are your own, but other owners have faced decisions like yours. They’ve had to figure out when to hire, how to raise prices, and what to do when sales slow down. Hearing what they tried and how it worked out can help you see options you hadn’t considered.

Who leads the meetings?

Jay Grosman facilitates the founding St. Louis chapter. He keeps the conversation focused, makes sure every owner gets time to speak, and helps members leave with a clear next step.

How many owners are in a chapter?

Each chapter is a small, private group. We keep it small so every owner has time to bring a challenge, hear from the group, and share what they’ve learned.

When and where do you meet?

The founding St. Louis chapter meets online once a month for three and a half hours. The chapter also gets together in person. We’ll share the meeting schedule during the application process so you can check it against your calendar.

What do you talk about in a meeting?

Members bring decisions they’re facing right now. You might be deciding whether to hire, change your pricing, or hand off work you’ve been doing yourself. The group asks questions and shares relevant experience. You choose your next step and report back at the next meeting. We’ll also bring in guest speakers and set aside time for focused discussions on specific business challenges.

What’s the cost and commitment?

Founding membership is $497 per month with a six-month minimum commitment. Membership centers on the monthly Boardroom meetings. Private business coaching is available separately through Elevating Business Solutions.

What if I have to miss a meeting?

We ask members to make the monthly meeting a priority. If something comes up and you can’t attend, let us know ahead of time. When you return, be ready to share where things stand on your commitments.

What do you expect from members?

Show up, participate, and keep the discussion confidential. Bring real challenges, share your experience without pitching your services, and follow through on the steps you commit to.

Do I need special equipment to join online?

A reliable internet connection, a camera, a microphone, and a private place to speak freely are all you need.
