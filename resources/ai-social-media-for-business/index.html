How to Plan a Month of Social Media Content with AI

Start with your audience and voice, create content in batches, and review it before scheduling.

Begin with the customer you want to reach.

Describe who you help, what questions they ask, and what makes your business useful to them. Add your brand voice, photos, and relevant business information.

Create a batch, then review it.

The EBS AI social media platform can generate 10 posts in seconds. Check captions and visuals for accuracy, relevance, and voice. Remove claims you cannot support and avoid sharing private customer information.

Plan the month around your business.

Generate additional batches as needed. Mix useful answers, examples of your work, and timely updates. A month of content does not need to mean posting every day.

Schedule and learn.

Choose publishing dates, check your connected accounts, and review performance. Consistent posting does not guarantee leads; the message, audience, and next step still matter.
