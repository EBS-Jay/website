Your Questions, Answered.

Where should a small business start with AI?

Start with a task or process that repeatedly consumes time or creates missed handoffs. Define the business problem before choosing a tool. EBS helps evaluate whether AI or a simpler process change makes sense.

Does EBS build the AI solutions?

EBS offers advice and connects you with partners for implementation when needed. Implementation scope and services are discussed with the partner; advice does not automatically include building a solution.

Will AI replace my employees?

Our focus is helping people work more efficiently. Suitable uses may include draft responses, summaries, information retrieval, and repetitive workflows, with human review where appropriate.

Can we improve tools we already use?

Yes. Reviewing existing tools and workflows is part of deciding what to change. A new tool is not always the most useful next step.
