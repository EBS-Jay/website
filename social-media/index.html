Your Questions, Answered.

How can I create a month of social media content?

The platform generates batches of 10 posts in seconds. Create batches to build your month of content, review the captions and visuals, and schedule them. A month of content does not necessarily mean 30 individual posts.

Can I edit posts before they publish?

Yes. Captions and designs are editable. Review the wording, images, and schedule before content goes live.

How many social media accounts are included?

Silver includes one social media account, Gold includes up to three, and Platinum includes all supported social media accounts. All three plans include platform features and monthly coaching.

Which social platforms can I connect?

Account connections depend on the platforms currently supported by the product and the type of account you have. During your demonstration, we will check the specific accounts you want to connect.

Do you write and manage every post for me?

The platform helps generate content that you can review and schedule. Silver includes training so you can configure your voice. Gold and Platinum include full initial setup by EBS. These packages describe platform access, coaching, and support; they do not promise a fully managed social media agency service.
