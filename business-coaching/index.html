Practical answers before we talk

Your Questions, Answered.

Who is EBS business coaching for?

Our focus is established business owners who want help with people issues, sales, processes, priorities, or growth. Businesses older than five years are welcome.

What do we work on during coaching?

Bring a current challenge or decision. We examine what is causing it, consider options, agree on practical next steps, and return to what happened. Topics can include accountability, sales follow-up, hiring decisions, and SOPs.

How is coaching different from the Roundtable?

Coaching is one-on-one work focused on your business. The Roundtable is a monthly peer group where selected owners contribute perspectives and hold one another accountable.

Do I need a business plan before contacting EBS?

You can start with the challenge you are facing. Tell us what is happening, what you have tried, and what you want to improve.

Does coaching guarantee revenue growth?

No. Progress depends on your business, the decisions made, and the work carried out. We focus on practical actions and reviewing what changes.
