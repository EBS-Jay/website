Membership by application

The Right People Make the Room.

This is a working group for established business owners ready to contribute, speak candidly, and follow through.

We review each application for business experience, commitment to monthly meetings, and compatibility with the group. We avoid placing direct competitors together.

Applying does not guarantee acceptance. The goal is a room where members can trust one another and do meaningful work.
